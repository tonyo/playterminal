About PlayTerminal

PlayTerminal is an experimental project which aims at bringing nice old-school terminal/console games back to life. And where? Right in your browser! This level of awesomeness became possible thanks to a lightweight container technology called Docker. It is also powered by a secret recipe cloud backend called Root&Roll, which you can already experience as a part of Stepik Linux challenges. Stay tuned for updates!
