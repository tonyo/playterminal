Greed is a strategy game: try to eat as much as possible of the board before munching yourself into a corner.

The board is a grid of numbers, and you are the @. When you move in a direction, you erase N squares that way, N being the first number in that direction. Your score is the total number of squares eaten. Greed will not let you move off the grid or over a square you have already eaten, and the game ends when no valid moves are left.

Greed was written by Matt Day, whose last version came out in 1990, and is now maintained by Eric S. Raymond.

Move with h, j, k, l and the diagonals y, u, b, n, or with the arrow keys. Press p to highlight the possible moves and q to quit.
