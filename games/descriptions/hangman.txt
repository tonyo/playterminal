The computer thinks of a word and the player tries to guess it by suggesting letters, within a certain number of guesses.

"The origins of Hangman are obscure meaning not discovered, but it seems to have arisen in Victorian times," says Tony Augarde, author of The Oxford Guide to Word Games.

This is Hangman from the BSD games collection, with an American English word list. Each wrong letter adds a piece to the gallows, and the man is hanged when the drawing is complete.

Type a letter to guess it. Press space for the next word and Enter to quit.
