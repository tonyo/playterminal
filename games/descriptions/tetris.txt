All-time classic: a famous puzzle game which was ported to nearly every toaster available. Tetriminos FTW!

Move and rotate the falling pieces to fill whole rows, which then disappear. The pieces fall faster with every level, and the game ends when the stack reaches the top.

This is Micro Tetris by Joachim Wiberg, based on John Tromp's 1989 entry to the International Obfuscated C Code Contest.

Move with j and l, rotate with k, and press space to drop. Press p to pause and q to quit.
